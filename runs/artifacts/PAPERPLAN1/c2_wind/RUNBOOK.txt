    again be confused in the artifacts.

STEP 4  ACCEPTANCE — what proves the wind actually acted
---------------------------------------------------------------------
BASELINE, measured today on the three existing no-wind sorties
(evidence/wind_absence_probe.json):
    median(groundspeed - airspeed) = +0.44 m/s (Pioneer, Va 21.5)
                                     +0.38 m/s (HERO,    Va 19.0)
    -> heading-INDEPENDENT and proportional to airspeed (0.44/21.5 = 2.05 %,
       0.38/19.0 = 2.00 %): a true-airspeed vs reported-airspeed bias, NOT wind.
       ⚠ its exact origin is NOT established here — ISA density at 220 m MSL
       would give only ~1.1 %, and this repo already has a note about SITL
       airspeed/density (memory: sitl-airspeed-double-density).  Whatever it
       is, it is a constant multiplier and must be subtracted (or, better,
       compared cell-to-cell) before any delta is read as wind.
    east-minus-west asymmetry = 0.00 - 0.02 m/s over 10 aircraft
    -> implied along-EW wind <= 0.01 m/s.  The sim had no wind, full stop.

AFTER a 5 m/s / 270 deg sortie, ALL FOUR must hold or the cell is void:
  (a) wind_readback.json: 15/15 rows ok (SIM_WIND_SPD 5, DIR 270, TURB 0, T 1,

WIND DIRECTION MATTERS AS MUCH AS SPEED (evidence/planner_wind_crosswind_dirs.json).
The scanning strips of this scenario run EAST-WEST (in HV4SITL16's flight.csv,
383 of A1's 458 cruise samples are on east- or west-bound tracks), so a 270 deg
wind is PURELY ALONG-TRACK: it exercises only the parallel term of the wind
triangle, and the two directions cancel in the totals — exactly the reviewer's
"east-west errors may cancel".  Planned, same scenario, 5 m/s:
    from 270 (along strips): makespan 1260.4 s  30.658 km  min_sep_h 105.46 m
    from 180 (across strips): makespan  966.2 s  33.509 km  min_sep_h 322.22 m
    from   0 (across strips): makespan 1221.0 s  31.697 km  min_sep_h  96.52 m
                              (legal: min_sep_v 60.0 m >= dh_sep 50 m)
=> recommend ONE extra cell, HV4W55S: plan 5 @180 / sim 5 @180, which is the
   only cell that tests the perpendicular (crab-angle) term at all:
