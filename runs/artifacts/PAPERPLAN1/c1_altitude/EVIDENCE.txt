ROOT CAUSE (verified, not inferred)
  hv_dccpp/ardupilot.py:388-395  reduce_waypoints() re-discretises every arc into a
  circumscribed polygon and gives each NEW vertex an ENU z by LINEAR interpolation
  between the arc endpoints (line 391 `zs = z[i0] + frac * dz`), and an `agl` field by
  the same linear interpolation (line 393).  A constant-AGL surface is a paraboloid in
  the local tangent-plane ENU frame (z = h - d^2/2R_earth), and a strip-end U-turn
  carries the track 195-300 m further from the frame origin than its own endpoints, so
  the interpolated z sits above the 120 m AGL surface at the vertex's own distance.
  100 % (38/38, 13/13, 37/37) of the >120 m exported items are such vertices; the max
  over non-vertex items is 119.999987 m.  The `agl` field still reads 120.0000, so the
  validator cannot see it, and hv_dccpp/sim.py:58 `_AGL_TOL_M = 1.0` would forgive it
  anyway - and sim.py:732 checks plan.waypoints, never the exported item list.
