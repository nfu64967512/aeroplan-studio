    Full text checked (PMC6068989).  FINDINGS THAT CONSTRAIN THE WORDING:
      * The paper DOES model the ground track of a turn in wind as a trochoid, and
        builds Dubins-style paths from tangents between two trochoids.  Its turn
        parametrisation is the plain x_t = -R cos(alpha), y_t = R sin(alpha) + R*beta*alpha
        with R = V / psi_dot.
      * The paper does NOT inflate the turn radius by (1 + W/Va)^2 anywhere.
      * Its turning-time equations are attributed by the authors to two of their own
        references, one of which is Techy & Woolsey (see below).  The repo's own
        first-hand reading note says the same: docs/hv_dccpp/論文_三篇全文閱讀_2026-09-10.md:104
        ("轉彎時間模型 Eqs.(6)-(18) 他們自己註明出自 Techy & Woolsey [19]").
    => [5] is a CORRECT citation for "the ground track of a constant-bank turn in wind
       is a trochoid".  It is the WRONG citation for the scalar R_eff = R_theo(1+W/Va)^2.
       The paper must present R_eff as a one-line consequence of trochoid curvature, not
       as something taken from [5], and must not present it as new either.
