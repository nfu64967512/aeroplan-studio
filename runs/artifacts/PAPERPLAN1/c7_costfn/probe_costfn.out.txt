== 4. east-west cancellation (why a no-wind SITL still agreed with a 5 m/s plan) ==
  A/Pioneer Va=21.5: E leg Vg=26.500  W leg Vg=16.500 | one E+W pair over L=1560 m: wind 153.413 s vs calm 145.116 s = +5.718%
      single E leg alone: -18.87% , single W leg alone: +30.30%  (they nearly cancel in a pair)
  B/HERO    Va=19.0: E leg Vg=24.000  W leg Vg=14.000 | one E+W pair over L=1560 m: wind 176.429 s vs calm 164.211 s = +7.440%
      single E leg alone: -20.83% , single W leg alone: +35.71%  (they nearly cancel in a pair)
  -> residual is second order: 1/(Va-W) + 1/(Va+W) = 2Va/(Va^2-W^2) > 2/Va
